The main learning mode

Listen & Find

The child hears an animal’s name, looks at the pictures, and taps the matching animal. The response is immediate, clear, and encouraging.

Learning through sound and interaction

Short rounds help young children connect spoken words with pictures—without menus, pressure, or visual noise.

  • Builds early animal vocabulary
  • Develops listening and understanding
  • Trains attention and concentration
  • Celebrates correct answers with gentle praise
